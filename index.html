Sarkari Form Kit: Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.sarkariformkit.app

Summary

Sarkari Form Kit works fully offline. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your photos or any other data anywhere. We do not collect, store, share or sell personal data.

What the app handles on your phone

  • Camera. Used only when you tap “Take photo”, to capture your face, signature, thumb or hand impression, or handwritten declaration.
  • Photos you pick. A picture you choose from your gallery is opened only to prepare the file you asked for.
  • Name and date. If you type a name and date to print below a photo, they are stored only on your phone.
  • Saved files. Photos you choose to keep, and the files you export, stay in the app's private storage and in your phone's Downloads folder.

Face detection runs on your phone using a model bundled inside the app. Nothing is uploaded for processing.

What we do not do

  • No account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No data is shared with us or with any third party.
  • App data is excluded from Android cloud backup.

Deleting your data

Delete individual items from “Saved files” in the app, or clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Files you saved to Downloads stay there until you delete them in your file manager.

Children

The app is meant for students and job seekers filling exam forms. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Not a government app

Sarkari Form Kit is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government, ministry or exam body such as IBPS, SSC, UPSC, RRB or NTA, and it does not provide any government service. Always confirm the upload rules on the official portal.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
